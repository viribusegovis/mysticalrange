See exactly which blocks your **Mystical Agriculture Harvester** and **Mystical Automation Fertilizer** work on.

Both machines work on a square of crops in front of them, and that square grows with every machine upgrade. Neither machine shows you where it is. This mod adds a small toggle button to their screens that outlines the area in the world, sized to the upgrade currently installed.

Client-side only: no server install, nothing added to the world, no recipes or config.

## How to use it

1. Open a Harvester or Fertilizer.
2. Click the **□** button just left of the upgrade slot. It turns into **■** and a green outline appears in front of the machine.
3. Close the screen. The outline stays so you can plant or build around it.
4. Open the machine again and click **■** to hide it.

## What the outline follows

- **Upgrades:** swap the upgrade while the screen is open and the outline resizes right away.
- **Rotation:** turn the machine and the outline moves with it.
- **Breaking:** break the machine and its outline goes away.
- **Several machines:** you can show as many outlines at once as you like. They are cleared when you leave the world.

The area is the same one the machines use: a flat square, one block tall, at the machine's own height, directly in front of the side it faces. It is 3×3 with no upgrade and grows with each tier.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **26.1.2**, NeoForge **26.1.2.41-beta** or newer
- [Mystical Agriculture](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mystical-agriculture) 9.0.0 or newer (and its library, Cucumber)
- [Mystical Automation](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mystical-automation) is optional; you only need it for the Fertilizer

## Good to know

The upgrade size is read from the machine's screen, because the game doesn't tell your client when a machine's upgrade changes. If automation swaps an upgrade while the screen is closed, open the machine once to refresh the outline.
